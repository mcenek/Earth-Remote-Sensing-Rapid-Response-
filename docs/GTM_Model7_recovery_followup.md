# Train-only stability follow-up, fixed after first recovery ablation

The first recovery ablation is sealed under `outputs/GTM_Model7_recovery_20261023/train_diagnostics`. On unchanged models, weight 1 gave zero train recall. Weight 10 gave train recall but 21.6% to 58.8% false-positive area on negative development scenes. The residual's ordinary BCE tiny-crop loss diverged while Siamese fit those crops. No test label or prediction informed these observations.

Before this follow-up runs, the checks are fixed: original trainer and first recovery hashes remain unchanged; manifest validation reads test inputs only; reject populated output folders; stop on nonfinite loss/gradients; use the same 48-draw crop schedule and fixed 0.5 threshold; save initial and final train-probe losses, model checkpoints, train probabilities, full train and negative-development confusion, and source/configuration hashes. Fresh-process replay must reproduce train probabilities and confusion. No held-out prediction is produced.

The follow-up uses unchanged architectures and normalization. A residual tiny-crop stability trial uses ordinary masked BCE with learning rates `0.0001` and `0.0005`, 300 identical updates each. Two Siamese training arms use positive weights 2 and 3, learning rate `0.0005`, and the original 0.1 Dice term for 1800 common seeded updates. Record checkpoints at 360 and 1800 updates. Choose only from train recall, train IoU, and negative-development FPR. An arm that predicts all negative is ineligible. This trial tests optimizer/loss sensitivity, not positive generalization.
