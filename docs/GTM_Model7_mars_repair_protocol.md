# GTM Model 7 MARS temporal repair protocol

This protocol and its failure cases were written before the repaired cohort exporter. The source is the locally cached UNEP-IMEO MARS-S2L publication v1 release, with official `train.csv` and `test.csv` rows and the pinned `paper_v3_mixed_samples.jsonl` metadata. Only United States Sentinel-2 L1C observations enter this internal experiment. Official test rows may be repartitioned because this is an exploratory internal comparison, never an official MARS benchmark.

## Failure cases and safeguards

1. A CSV row lacks pinned metadata, its producer class, site, coordinates, sensor, split, or asset paths disagree, or a missing mask is interpreted as no plume. Stop or exclude with a recorded reason. `NO_PLUME` comes only from an explicit reviewed producer class.
2. A source image, target cloud mask, replacement reference image/cloud mask, or positive annotation is missing, differs in byte size, or fails its pinned upstream hash. Stop on changed assets. No downloads are permitted.
3. A target/reference image is not a 200 by 200, 12-band uint16 Sentinel-2 L1C asset with the declared B02/B03/B04/B08/B11/B12 and background six-band descriptions; a mask differs in CRS, transform, shape, dtype, or known values. Stop rather than warp or assume a channel order.
4. The release's embedded background scene may be cloudy or itself a plume. Always replace it, regardless of its apparent quality, with the first six bands from the closest observed image at least 24 hours earlier and on a different UTC calendar date, at the same official physical site and exact CSV grid, with producer-reviewed `NO_PLUME`, observability `clear`, producer clear percentage at least 90, and a date gap no more than 60 days. Require that candidate's target cloud mask and both cached assets. Ties use sample ID. A second acquisition only seconds earlier is not a temporal reference.
5. Candidate reference selection looks at source metadata only and occurs before inference. A reference observation must belong to the same 25 km/acquisition-connected component as the target; source identities are rechecked after partitioning. A later acquisition or cross-component asset is forbidden.
6. A current or reference pixel is cloudy, nonfinite, zero/nodata, or not supported by all six bands. The 20 m cell is valid only when all four native current and reference pixels meet that condition. Require at least 90% valid 20 m cells; record every positive/negative exclusion. Do not relax the threshold to fill a split.
7. A producer positive annotation is not binary, empty, off-grid, or has no supported 20 m positive cell. Exclude. A producer negative has a zero target only because it has an explicit `NO_PLUME` class. A 20 m target is positive if any of its four 10 m source pixels is positive; retain the 10 m native annotation and repeated strict validity.
8. A site within 25 km, physical site, target/reference acquisition, source path, or byte-identical spectral image appears across partitions. Form connected components over *all* 5,793 official US Sentinel-2 rows before raster reads, including candidate references and negative-only observations, and check the final scene and source identities again. Cloud or binary annotation TIFFs may legitimately have byte-identical contents at unrelated sites, so their hash is verified against its pinned upstream identity but not used alone as a cross-partition leak key.
9. A score, prior prediction, or test annotation influences model fitting, negative selection, threshold setting, or split assignment. Fix components, region allocation, seed, positive eligibility, and site-balanced negative limits first. The pinned mixed metadata contains a `paper_baseline` prior-prediction field; omit it from all exported source rows. The test is held out from the two newly trained models only; Kansas and Mississippi positives were already consumed in October 23 confirmation and prior publication evaluation.
10. The source catalog has only six positive components: main Permian, west Permian, and four one-positive distant sites. This is too small for a strong national test. Do not claim national generalization, untouched test data, or official benchmark status from the repaired MARS run.

## Partition and selection

The positive component allocation is fixed from metadata: train uses main Permian, south Texas, and Oklahoma; dev uses west Permian; test uses Mississippi and Kansas. The west Permian component was previously consumed and serves development/regression. Kansas and Mississippi were also previously consumed and serve exploratory held-out-from-new-model regression. All quality-passing positives with a supported earlier reference enter their assigned split.

Negative-only components are allocated deterministically by a fixed seed while keeping the entire connected component in one partition. Every selected negative must have the same earlier clear reviewed-negative reference contract and 90% bidate pixel support. Selection rounds across components and physical sites, with at most eight observations per physical site. The target limits are approximately 300 train, 80 dev, and 80 test negatives; fewer are acceptable when the fixed quality/source rules leave too few. Source quality and metadata determine selection, never model scores.

## Data contract

The new directory has `manifest.json` (schema `GTM_Model7_temporal_cohort_v1`), `receipt.json`, and one compressed NPZ per selected scene. A scene ID is `MARS_<sample_id>` and records split, connected group, official site ID, original CSV and pinned metadata row, selected earlier reference observation ID and acquisition/time, source asset identities, grids, and array hash. Source roles are `target_image`, `target_cloud`, `reference_image`, `reference_cloud`, and `plume_mask` for positive scenes.

Each NPZ contains `target_image` and `reference_image` as float32 `(100,100,6)` in B02/B03/B04/B08/B11/B12 order, `valid` as bool `(100,100)`, `target` as binary float32 `(100,100)`, `label_native` as uint8 `(200,200)`, and `valid_native` as bool `(200,200)`. Both images are plain 2 by 2 means from their own native target-date six bands. Native scoring repeats each 20 m model probability to its four original 10 m cells and scores only repeated strict validity. Trainers compute normalization from train scenes alone.

The exporter creates a new directory and never overwrites it. It records source hashes, selection/exclusion counts, positive sites, class pixels, date gaps, component membership, and no-download status. Its receipt is the repeatable preparation artifact. Raster QA and any model comparison must be performed after this metadata-first protocol is fixed.
