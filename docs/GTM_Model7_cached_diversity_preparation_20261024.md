# Cached plume diversity and reference preparation

This protocol precedes the new exporter. This pass prepares data, not a new model claim. Preserve every earlier dataset, checkpoint and evaluation result. No imagery downloads, relabeling, external journal writes, or training are required.

## Failures to prevent

1. Facility coordinates, a missing methane raster, or an unlabeled exterior become a methane-positive or clear label. Accept only explicit source plume/no-plume classes and positive binary producer annotations. CAFO locations remain a separate facility reference layer.
2. Repeated dates are described as independent sites. Report observations, physical sites, countries, sensors, original roles, and known US states separately.
3. Previously evaluated US scenes enter fitting through a supplement or its reference. Preserve all 565 scenes and their original assignments in the previous MARS clear manifest. Additional training must not share a site, acquisition, spectral hash, or location within 25 km of its DEV/TEST scenes. No new evaluation rasters are opened. Historical project exposure remains disclosed.
4. Landsat or L2A are silently treated as Sentinel-2 L1C. Inventory them separately. The directly compatible auxiliary export uses official TRAIN Sentinel-2 L1C only. International examples are auxiliary training for a US task, not evidence of US performance.
5. A nominal background composite is called a verified clear reference. References must be actual cached same-site and exact-grid observations, explicit reviewed NO_PLUME, source metadata clear and at least 90 percent clear, on an earlier different UTC day, 24 hours to 60 days before target. Use that observation's own six bands and own cloud mask.
6. The nearest candidate fails raster QA and the site is lost without checking alternatives. Try eligible references in increasing age, then ID. Choose the first passing the frozen both-date quality rules. Never select by model output, target plume contrast, annotation shape, or outcome.
7. A cloudy, snowy, empty, shifted-grid, corrupted, or wrongly described image enters fitting. Reuse the pinned source-hash verification, exact Sentinel band/grid/dtype checks, strict both-date cloud-code-zero support, at least 90 percent valid 20 m cells, and existing fixed snow-like gate. Record every attempted candidate and rejection. Target label content is used only to validate its source contract and supported positive cells, never to rank references.
8. Claims of perfect alignment replace visual inspection. Save actual target/reference/annotation previews and image-only pair diagnostics. A shared raster grid does not prove landmark alignment or matching illumination. Flag, rather than silently correct, suspicious inputs.
9. A huge supplementary export consumes storage or hides geographic imbalance. Country/site round-robin, at most 100 accepted positive sites, at most two positives and four no-plume targets per accepted site, all training only. Stop at 1 GiB of prepared arrays. Report unmet quotas honestly; do not relax QA. The source pool is cached official TRAIN only; no validation or test pixels are used to fill quotas.
10. The supplement is called ready without checking its combined use. Produce a new combined manifest retaining all old scene entries and arrays by verified paths, append accepted training-only scenes, verify source and prepared-array identities, acquisition/site/geographic/spectral-hash separation, label/shape/validity contracts, and show the actual inputs in a local HTML review. Preparation does not certify that the models will transfer.

## Repeatable acceptance

The new versioned output must include metadata-only eligibility and exclusion counts, reference attempt logs, source/protocol/exporter hashes, selected target and reference times, source grids, arrays, country and site counts, a compatible combined manifest, and an end-to-end receipt. Independently reconstruct every new selected array and label from cached TIFFs. Recheck previous DEV/TEST entries and old manifest hash. Save previews of all new positive sites and representative no-plume/reference failures. Root inspects the actual figures and local HTML. Supply exact preparation/verification and next training commands. Do not start another fit in this preparation pass.

## CAFO proximity queue, before implementation

Read the existing Iowa inventory and cached MARS/S2CM scene metadata only. Preserve stable inventory OBJECTIDs and all source row numbers; identical duplicate records can share a point, but conflicting coordinates for one OBJECTID must be flagged and excluded from nearest-point calculations. The inventory's undocumented datum is an explicit WGS84-like latitude/longitude assumption for candidate matching only. Check point ranges. Do not collapse different facility IDs at the same coordinate.

Write a separate candidate queue for existing methane sites within 2 km and 5 km of an unambiguous inventory point, with the exact great-circle distance, inventory status, input dataset/split, original coordinate and source hashes. These fixed distances describe proximity only, not detection accuracy or a methane label. Do not change any dataset split, mask, reference choice, or model feature from this queue. A zero-match result is valid. Check the nearest-distance computation against an independent scalar haversine calculation and preserve the receipt. This metadata-only check does not require opening held-out images or labels.
