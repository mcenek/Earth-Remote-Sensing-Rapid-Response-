# CAFO Pass 1 label readiness (2026-09-11)

## Decision

The draft five-rotation inventory is usable as an acquisition and facility-retrieval sampling frame after source/coordinate decisions, but it is not a supervised methane training set. The local artifacts contain no reviewed facility-negative controls and no date-specific plume presence/absence labels aligned to these CAFO records. Therefore all five rotations cannot currently support a defensible trained methane classifier, plume detector, plume segmentation result, or methane precision/recall claim. A label-free or facility-only pilot endpoint remains feasible: crop-level Sentinel-2 observability/coverage and known-facility retrieval, with detections treated as review candidates.

## Inventory and rotation evidence

Inputs inspected were `reports/acquisition/cafo_rotations_2026-09-11/summary.json`, its `facilities.csv`, the source CSV, and the existing CAFO research/coordinate reports. The plan has:

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| Source rows / unique facilities | 14,155 / 14,155; 12,703 listed Active and 1,452 Inactive |
| Draft cohort | 12,703 Active records, 4,244 connected components, 2 km grouping |
| Rotation direction | Five rotations; one fold for training (~20%), other four for validation (~80%) |
| Fold facility counts | 2,541 / 2,541 / 2,541 / 2,540 / 2,540 |
| Plan status | `planning_only_not_frozen_scientific_split` |
| Source semantics | `known facility inventory, no methane labels` |

The largest component is `iowa_cafo_group_0006`, with 1,770 facilities, all assigned to fold 0. Its extent in the draft output is latitude 42.82223–43.50012 and longitude -96.54026–-95.65204 (roughly 75 km north-south by 73 km east-west at Iowa latitudes). It contains 1,769 `IA` rows and one `MN` row; all 1,770 rows are listed `Active`. Consequently, rotation 0's training pool is dominated by this one connected component (1,770 of 2,541 facilities, about 70%), and the other rotations validate against it. This is a material dependence and representativeness issue; the component should not be split just to balance counts. The crop-width and grouping assumptions also need reconfirmation before a scientific freeze.

The three catalog probes are metadata-only discovery receipts. They returned candidate Sentinel-2 L2A items for three active facilities, but no crop-level visibility/QA, retained training image, facility review, or methane label. The 2024 query year is explicitly provisional.

## Coordinate accuracy and status meaning

The source has an `Accuracy` field with 16 numeric codes, dominated by 20 (10,537 rows), 570 (1,574), 0 (1,282), 25 (322), and 280 (317). No local schema, CRS/datum declaration, unit, lookup table, or documentation maps these values to metres, confidence, horizontal error, or another accuracy meaning. The coordinate audit therefore correctly leaves `Accuracy` semantics unknown. `Latitude`/`Longitude` are parseable and `X_Coord`/`Y_Coord` look UTM-like, but neither CRS/datum nor UTM zone is declared.

`opStatus` is a source inventory status snapshot, not an observation-date methane label. `Active` does not establish activity at a 2024 overpass, and `Inactive` does not establish no plume. The `State` field similarly describes the record; it is not a methane or facility-control class. `collectBy`, `ColDate`, `refPntTxt`, `VerifyTxt`, and `locComment` provide provenance/context for some rows but do not supply plume adjudication.

## Label/control search

No local CAFO-aligned reviewed facility-negative control table, control manifest, analyst review record, or date-specific plume annotation was found. The only CAFO-specific tables are the source inventory and the derived rotation output. Existing EMIT/MethaneUnion, MARS, STARCOP, JPL, and Stanford artifacts describe other cohorts or protocols; they do not establish labels for these CAFO coordinates and were not used as substitutes. The catalog probe records contain scene metadata only. Absence from a catalog, absence of a plume asset, `Inactive` status, cloud filtering, or being unlisted as a CAFO must not be converted into a negative class.

This leaves the label inventory as follows:

| Proposed target | Availability for these CAFOs | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Facility inventory / location | Available for the provisional Active cohort | Supports sampling, coverage, and facility-association/retrieval accounting |
| Reviewed facility-positive/negative classification labels | Not found | Cannot report facility-class precision/recall or train a standard binary facility classifier |
| Date-specific plume presence/absence | Not found | Cannot train or evaluate date-specific methane detection |
| Dense plume masks / methane intensity | Not found | Cannot train segmentation/regression or report AP/IoU/intensity error |
| Crop-level observability/quality | Not yet available; must be generated by a bounded pilot | Supports inclusion/abstention and coverage denominators, not methane truth |

## Concrete blockers to all five rotations

1. There is no target label for the intended methane task at the CAFO observation/date level.
2. There are no independently reviewed facility negatives/controls, so facility recovery cannot be converted into classification precision or false-positive rate.
3. Sentinel-2 scenes have not been acquired or crop-QA-verified; the existing candidates are L2A and require an adapter, while frozen methane benchmarks expect a verified input/product contract.
4. The coordinate source is not confirmed as Huang's file, its CRS/datum and `Accuracy` semantics are unresolved, and the provisional 2024 window does not prove historical facility activity.
5. The 1,770-row component creates a dominant, correlated fold and the 2 km grouping was designed for a proposed crop size, not yet validated for the final footprint.

## Feasible endpoint without new labels

Proceed only as a bounded engineering/facility-retrieval pilot: select predeclared sites across folds and coordinate-quality codes, acquire/inspect crop-level SCL/cloud/nodata and georeferencing, report attempted/retained/abstained observations and known-facility coverage among observable sites, and preserve group/fold IDs. A frozen methane model may be run only as an input-compatible diagnostic; its outputs are unverified candidate plumes and must not be scored as CAFO positives or negatives. Unlisted detections remain unclassified pending source review.

Before any five-rotation supervised run, obtain (a) authoritative coordinate provenance/CRS and accuracy documentation, (b) independently reviewed facility controls, and (c) date-specific plume evidence or explicit reviewed no-plume observations with a declared sensor/time/observability contract. Until then, Pass 1 is label-blocked for methane training, while the acquisition and coverage endpoint remains feasible.

Audit scope was local and read-only; no imagery was downloaded, no historical test outcomes were opened, and no existing code or data was changed.
